## Brand & Style

This design system frames an upscale, cheerful boutique for heirloom-grade plush companions. It merges high-end artisanal craftsmanship with comforting, child-like delight. The aesthetic balances warmth, warmth-induced comfort, and a clean editorial luxury tone—avoiding cluttered toy-store chaotic tropes in favor of an inviting, luminous boutique experience.

The design movement blends **warm tactile minimalism** with **soft neo-editorial touches**:
- **Tactile Softness:** Elements mimic plush surfaces via gentle, pillowy radii, pill-shaped interactions, and ultra-diffused sunny ambient shadows.
- **Luminous Sun-Drenched Canvas:** Generous negative space anchored in warm ivory and clotted cream layers rather than stark, cold clinical whites.
- **Editorial Chic:** Balanced proportions and generous leading ensure that the playfulness remains premium, calm, and curated.

## Colors

The color system establishes a joyful, honeyed, sunlit baseline punctuated by high-spirit bubblegum pink moments.

### Roles and Application
- **Primary Brand (`#FFD52A`):** The signature vibrant sunshine yellow. Reserved for high-profile badges, primary CTAs, key brand markers, and joyful highlights.
- **Secondary Accent (`#FF3D79`):** Bubblegum pink. Applied sparingly for delightful micro-interactions, "Add to Wishlist" states, sale tags, and urgent interactive cues.
- **Tertiary & Tonal Layers:**
  - `Warm Butter Yellow (#FFF0A8)`: Ideal for soft container backgrounds, gentle alerts, and interactive hover fills.
  - `Soft Golden Yellow (#FFC928)`: Deepening shade for active states and rich structural highlights.
  - `Blush Pink (#FFE0E9)`: Subtle backdrops for pink accent icons, tooltips, and soft decorative badges.
- **Surfaces & Grounds:**
  - `Main Background (#FFFDF5)`: Warm ivory canvas providing luminous comfort without the sterile bite of standard pure white.
  - `Supporting Surface (#FFF7D9)`: Pale buttery cream for alternating product rows, secondary trays, and soft footers.
  - `Card & Floating Container Surface (#FFFFFF)`: Pure clean white, allowing plush imagery to retain true-to-life fur texture and authentic color fidelity.
- **Typography & Structure:**
  - `Main Typography (#513526)`: Deep cocoa brown. Ensures high contrast across yellow and ivory backgrounds while feeling fundamentally softer and richer than pitch black.
  - `Secondary Typography (#65564E)`: Warm charcoal for metadata, subtitles, and input placeholder hints.

## Typography

The typographic strategy pairs the round, approachable warmth of **Quicksand** for key headers and displays with the refined, crisp geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body copy and navigational controls.

### Implementation Rules
- Keep headings conversational, welcoming, and set in title case or sentence case. Avoid all-caps transformations in Quicksand to preserve its friendly, organic letterforms.
- Restrict all paragraph runs and intricate checkout text to Plus Jakarta Sans for rapid legibility.
- In mobile viewports, transition large display items strictly through `display-lg-mobile` and `headline-lg-mobile` to maintain airy vertical flow.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts a flexible 12-column grid on desktop screens, consolidating into 8 columns on tablet devices and 4 columns on mobile viewports.

### Rhythm & Constraints
- **Desktop Breakpoint (≥1200px):** 12-column fluid grid, max container width of 1320px, 40px outer margins (`margin`), and 24px column gutters (`gutter`).
- **Tablet Breakpoint (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout, 24px outer margins, and 16px column gutters.
- **Mobile Breakpoint (≤767px):** 4-column layout, 20px outer margins (`margin-sm`), and 16px column gutters (`gutter-sm`).
- **Component Breathing Space:** Cards and interactive clusters prioritize generous vertical paddings (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to preserve the airy luxury boutique atmosphere.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids dark or sterile grey drop shadows. Depth is achieved via **warm, honey-tinted ambient glows** and **nested tonal surfaces**.

### Elevation Scales
- **Ground Floor (Flat):** Native ivory (`#FFFDF5`) and pale cream (`#FFF7D9`) surfaces sit flush with zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Tiers):** Clean white cards float above the warm ivory canvas with an ambient shadow:  
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(81, 53, 38, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(255, 213, 42, 0.12);`
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Containers):**  
  `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -6px rgba(81, 53, 38, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(255, 213, 42, 0.20);`
- **Level 3 (Modals, Slide-over Carts, Floating Toasts):**  
  `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(81, 53, 38, 0.14), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(255, 61, 121, 0.08);`

### Border Accents
Borders are never harsh. When structural outlines are needed, apply a semi-transparent warm butter tone: `1px solid rgba(255, 213, 42, 0.35)` or a whisper of cocoa tint: `1px solid rgba(81, 53, 38, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **pill-shaped language (`level 3`)** that mirrors the huggable, organic curves of luxury plush toys.

### Geometry Guidelines
- **Buttons, Badges, and Chips:** Full pill contour (`border-radius: 9999px` or minimum `1rem / 16px`).
- **Product Cards & Panels:** Generously rounded at `1.5rem` (24px) to `2rem` (32px), creating soft, pillow-like frames for imagery.
- **Dialogs & Sheet Drawers:** Curvature of `2rem` (32px) on all exposed corners.
- Avoid razor-sharp 90-degree apexes on any user-facing surface.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Full pill shape, filled with Sunshine Yellow (`#FFD52A`), Deep Cocoa Brown text (`#513526`), bold typography (`label-lg`). Hover shifts to Soft Golden Yellow (`#FFC928`) with an ambient warm glow.
- **Accent CTA (e.g., "Adopt Now", "Special Gift"):** Full pill shape, Bubblegum Pink (`#FF3D79`) background with clean white typography.
- **Secondary Button:** Warm Butter Yellow (`#FFF0A8`) background with Deep Cocoa Brown text; or transparent with a 1.5px Deep Cocoa Brown border.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- Pure clean white (`#FFFFFF`) interior surface with 24px (`rounded-lg`) to 32px (`rounded-xl`) corner radius.
- Padding set to `1.5rem` (`space-lg`).
- Plush photography is framed on gentle, warm-tinted circular or rounded backdrops inside the card (`#FFF7D9` or `#FFE0E9`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Tag Badges (e.g., "100% Cashmere Plush", "Organic Cotton"):** Pill shape, Blush Pink (`#FFE0E9`) ground with Bubblegum Pink (`#FF3D79`) text, or Warm Butter Yellow (`#FFF0A8`) ground with Deep Cocoa Brown text.
- Micro-labels set in `label-sm` with slight uppercase tracking.

### Inputs & Form Controls
- **Fields:** Pure white surface, pill or soft-rounded (16px) contour, padded with `1rem` horizontal comfort.
- **Border:** 1.5px subtle cocoa tint (`rgba(81, 53, 38, 0.12)`).
- **Focus State:** 2px solid Sunshine Yellow (`#FFD52A`) border ring accompanied by a diffused 4px yellow ambient glow (`rgba(255, 213, 42, 0.3)`).
- **Placeholder:** Warm charcoal (`#65564E`) at 60% opacity.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkboxes feature rounded corners (6px); radios are circular.
- Unselected: Warm ivory surface with a gentle cocoa border.
- Selected: Filled in Bubblegum Pink (`#FF3D79`) with a crisp white checkmark/dot.

### Specialty Boutique Component: Plush Adoption Certificate
- A distinctive card container styled with an ivory inner background (`#FFFDF5`), a double-stroke warm gold border (`#FFD52A`), rounded 32px corners, and an authentic embossed badge motif in Bubblegum Pink and Gold.
